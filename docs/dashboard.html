Inputs Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2 surface temperature, delivered on a 30 m grid — the TIRS thermal sensor acquires at 100 m and USGS resamples it, which is why we treat small roofs as mixed pixels · Sentinel-2 L2A albedo on a 20 m grid, Bonafoni & Şekertekin narrow-to-broadband · NASA EMIT imaging spectrometer, 230 bands (60 m) · OpenStreetMap building footprints, vector. All free and open; no credentials needed. The basemap is not a tile service — it is our own Sentinel-2 albedo composite, so the roads and plot boundaries are measured reflectance.
Scope The map and the table cover the 500 roofs whose outlines ship with the project — the top 500 of 3,603 by total cooling benefit. Percentages are always measured against all 3,603 roofs. Under Most total cooling the ranking matches the published district-wide ranking exactly; the other two objectives re-rank within these 500.
Estimates Temperature drops use the measured albedo–temperature slope of −5.86 °C per unit albedo (95% CI −7.54 to −4.35), fitted with 2 km spatial block cross-validation. They are modelled estimates of surface temperature, not measurements of air temperature or of energy bills.
